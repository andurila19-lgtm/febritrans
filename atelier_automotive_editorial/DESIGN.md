## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an elevated, tactile automotive editorial experience tailored for high-end vehicle rentals in Madiun and East Java. Moving decisively away from utilitarian booking tools and SaaS dashboards, the aesthetic evokes the prestige of curated automotive monographs, bespoke concierge journals, and luxury marque showcases.

The personality balances commanding presence with quiet, disciplined restraint. Visual rhythm relies on deliberate asymmetrical compositions, expansive negative space, and meticulous hairline separations rather than decorative noise. The interface treats every vehicle—from executive luxury sedans and premium MPVs to high-displacement touring motorbikes—as a piece of industrial art. Interactions should feel deliberate, quiet, and refined, evoking precision mechanical instruments and prestige concierge service.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from atelier showrooms, burnished metals, and fine archival book stock.

- **Foundational Grounds:** The primary canvas alternates between warm chalk (`#FAF9F6`) and pure showroom white (`#FFFFFF`), avoiding sterile blue-whites. Secondary architectural containers and spec tables utilize warm stone (`#EFECE6` and `#E8E5DF`).
- **High-Contrast Anchors:** Deep obsidian charcoal (`#121314`) and carbon soot (`#1E1F21`) establish decisive typographic hierarchy and command inverted evening-mode showcases.
- **Burnished Accent:** Warm racing bronze (`#C89D42`) serves exclusively as an accent for status tags, curated badges, precision focus indicators, and key transactional triggers. It must never dominate surface areas.
- **Hairline Framing:** Subtle stone borders (`#E2DFD8` on light surfaces, `#2A2B2E` on dark surfaces) form crisp architectural divisions without introducing visual weight.
- **Functional Semantics:**
  - Success / Instant Availability: Deep hunter green (`#274736`) with pale sage ground (`#EBF0EC`).
  - Warning / Limited Inventory: Muted sienna (`#8E4D26`) with light bisque ground (`#F8EFE9`).
  - System Alert: Refined carmine (`#942A2A`).

## Typography

The typographical pairing forms the core of the editorial luxury voice.

- **Headlines & Editorial Statements (Newsreader):** Used for sweeping heroic statements, marque introductions, vehicle model titles, and large rate numerals. It lends authoritative cadence, literary warmth, and European coachbuilder tradition. Display weights lean regular (`400`) and medium italic (`500`) to evoke classic bespoke catalog layout.
- **Body & Information Architecture (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers clarity and balanced geometric structure for mechanical specs, booking terms, rental logistics, and functional inputs. Its modern humanist construction ensures high legibility on dark and light surfaces alike.
- **Editorial Micro-Typing:** Section identifiers, category headers, and technical labels are set in uppercase (`label-caps`) with deliberate wide tracking (`0.14em`) to establish crisp visual milestones throughout the layout.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout relies on an architectural 12-column grid system paired with strict horizontal rhythm.

- **Grid Discipline:** A 12-column configuration dynamically accommodates asymmetric 7/5, 8/4, and 9/3 spreads for vehicle showcases and narrative profiles. On mobile viewports (< 768px), the layout compresses to a disciplined 4-column system with edge-to-edge full-bleed imagery bordered by explicit inline padding.
- **Negative Space Philosophy:** Whitespace is treated as a premium material. Ample vertical rhythm (`space-3xl` to `space-editorial`) separates content chapters, allowing vehicle silhouettes and technical specs room to breathe without crowding.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: 360px – 767px (Gutters: `1.25rem`, Margins: `1.25rem`)
  - Tablet: 768px – 1023px (Gutters: `1.5rem`, Margins: `2rem`)
  - Desktop: 1024px – 1440px (Gutters: `2rem`, Margins: `3rem`, Max Width: `1360px`)
  - Widescreen: > 1440px (Centered container with dynamic structural side margins)

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately excludes drop shadows, floating blurred layers, and generic software elevation tokens. Visual depth is established through tonal layering, structural framing, and deliberate light modulation:

- **Tonal Stepping:** Surfaces stack through distinct matte backgrounds (`#FAF9F6` base canvas → `#FFFFFF` cards → `#EFECE6` specification plates).
- **Hairline Framing:** Crisp 1px structural outlines (`#E2DFD8`) bind cards, tables, and photography modules. Elevated elements do not rise along the Z-axis; they acquire crisp tonal contrast or dark charcoal enclosures (`#121314`).
- **Inverted Dark Editorial Spreads:** Deep Obsidian sections (`#121314`) anchor the base of the page or frame the flagship fleet carousel, creating cinematic weight without simulated physical drop-shadows.
- **Hover Transitions:** Interactive components react via razor-sharp hairline borders shifting from stone (`#E2DFD8`) to dark charcoal (`#121314`) or burnished bronze (`#C89D42`), paired with subtle image scale shifts (1.0 to 1.02x within masked boundaries).

## Shapes

The design system employs an uncompromising, sharp architectural corner language (`roundedness: 0`). 

- **Sharp Geometry:** Buttons, cards, vehicle specification cells, modal frames, and inputs feature strict right-angled edges (`0px` border-radius). This architectural precision echoes fine automotive bodywork, technical blueprinting, and classic editorial print framing.
- **Selective Micro-Softening:** The sole exception to total sharp geometry is internal status dots and circular icon glyph indicators, which remain true geometric circles (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Dividers & Lines:** Structural rules never exceed 1px solid thickness, emphasizing craftsmanship, poise, and laser-aligned layout balance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Obsidian black background (`#121314`), warm off-white typography (`#FAF9F6`), sharp corners (`0px`), zero shadow, and 1px border (`#121314`). On hover, ground transitions to rich burnished bronze (`#C89D42`) with instantaneous crisp color transition.
- **Secondary / Ghost CTA:** Transparent background, 1px hairline border (`#121314` or `#E2DFD8`), obsidian text. On hover, fills with light stone (`#EFECE6`).
- **Editorial Text Action:** Uppercase tracked link (`label-caps`) underscored with a permanent hairline rule 4px beneath the text. On hover, the rule turns racing bronze (`#C89D42`).

### Vehicle Showcase Cards
- **Architecture:** Crisp white canvas (`#FFFFFF`) bound by 1px stone border (`#E2DFD8`). Uncompromised sharp corners.
- **Aspect Ratio & Photography:** Vehicle photography sits inside a 16:10 or 3:2 bounding container with an off-white backdrop (`#F8F7F4`), masked cleanly with no border radius.
- **Data Layout:** Marque and model rendered in Newsreader (`headline-md`). Technical metadata (Transmission, Engine/Power, Fuel, Passenger Capacity) aligned horizontally in clean monospace or tracked sans tabular cells divided by hairline strokes.
- **Pricing Lockup:** Rate per 24 hours displayed with Newsreader numerals (`spec-numeral`) accompanied by muted label-caps (`IDR / DAY` or `DENGAN SUPIR`).

### Filter Chips & Fleet Selectors
- **Structure:** Crisp architectural tabs with sharp corners, 1px border.
- **Active State:** Obsidian fill (`#121314`) with white typography.
- **Inactive State:** Chalk ground (`#FAF9F6`), stone outline (`#E2DFD8`), muted charcoal text (`#6E6B65`). On hover, border shifts to obsidian black.

### Form Inputs & Date/Time Selectors
- **Treatment:** Architectural inset fields. Minimal flat ground (`#FFFFFF`), bounded by 1px `#E2DFD8` border.
- **Label Placement:** Floating label-caps (`10px`, tracked uppercase) positioned above the field.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid burnished bronze border (`#C89D42`) with zero glow or fuzzy focus ring.

### Spec Sheets & Comparison Tables
- **Grid:** Alternating hairline rows with stone backdrop (`#FAF9F6`) on header rows.
- **Typographic Treatment:** Specification titles in light charcoal (`#6E6B65`), mechanical values in bold charcoal (`#121314`).

### Concierge Guarantee Badges
- **Display:** Compact, razor-sharp tags featuring micro-dot indicators for vehicle status (e.g., "UNIT READY MADIUN", "DENGAN PENGEMUDI", "LEPAS KUNCI VERIFIED"). Burnished bronze accent borders reserved for signature VIP fleet classifications.
